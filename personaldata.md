# Music Listening History

## Description

I want to document my music listening habits since 2018. Music is something I keep on constantly, and my taste has changed over the last eight years, from EDM, rap, and pop to almost purely classical.

## Types of Data

- Song title
- Artist
- Date
- Listening duration

## Limitations

This data is only from Spotify and does not include my listening on other platforms, such as SoundCloud and YouTube.

It would also be difficult to measure my relationship with music through listening statistics alone. The number of times I listen to a song does not necessarily reflect how much I like it, as sometimes I leave a piece looping while I am studying or reading. These instances may distort the data, particularly when measuring the total number of plays of each song.
