# Music Listening History

## Description

I want to document my music listening habits since 2018. Music is something I keep on constantly, and I have noticed that over the last eight years, my taste has moved from EDM, rap, and pop to almost purely classical.

## Data Collection

I would collect the following information:

- Song title
- Artist
- Date and time
- Listening duration
- Number of plays
- Genre

I would use my Spotify extended listening history as the source of my data and organize it into a spreadsheet, with each listening event recorded separately.

## Limitations

This data would only be from Spotify, and would not include my listening on other platforms, such as SoundCloud and YouTube.

Genre could also be difficult to categorize, as some songs might belong to several genres, and different sources may categorize the same music differently.

Lastly, the number of plays does not always reflect preference, as I sometime leave a piece looping while I study or read.
