## Research Question
I originally wanted to look at how my music taste has changed since 2018, from mostly EDM, rap, and pop to almost entirely classical music.

After some feedback from Dr. Graham, I decided to focus more on the physical side of listening, rather than just the music itself.

My new research question is:

How has the material infrastructure through which I listen to music changed since 2018, and how has that shaped my listening?

## Data Collection

My main source of data is my Spotify extended listening history, which contains records of individual listening events going back to 2018.

I also want to try to work out what devices I was using during this period, including phones, computers, headphones, and speakers.

For devices I no longer have, I might be able to find old photographs, receipts, or emails showing when I owned them. Otherwise I will have to rely on my memory.

The problem is that knowing when I owned something doesn't necessarily tell me when or how often I used it to listen to music.

## Data Processing

I still need to look more closely at the Spotify data to see whether it includes any information about the devices I was using.

If it doesn't, I will have to try to connect the listening records with the information I can find elsewhere.

I will also need to distinguish between dates I can actually verify and dates I'm only estimating from memory.

## Problems and Decisions

The main change I've made so far is moving away from just studying changes in musical taste.

I realized that even though Spotify records what I listened to and when, this doesn't necessarily tell me very much about how I was listening.

For example, I might have played something repeatedly while studying without really paying attention to it. The data would still record those plays in the same way.

Another difficulty will be reconstructing which devices I used over the last eight years. I can probably establish approximately when I owned certain devices, but it will be harder to know how much I actually used them.

I also have listening history outside Spotify, particularly on YouTube and SoundCloud, which won't be included in the dataset.

For now, I need to find out how much information I can recover and what will have to remain uncertain.
