Receiving Dr. Graham's feedback on my PDAP has made me think more about the material side of something that I had mostly thought of as digital. I originally approached my Spotify listening history simply as data: song titles, artists, dates, listening time, etc., which I could use to look at how my music taste has changed. But thinking about it in terms of contemporary archaeology, there are also the material equipment and infrastructure necessary to make this listening possible. The Spotify listening history therefore documents only one layer of a much larger material system.

The project is consequently becoming less about simply tracking the change in my musical taste and more about asking what can actually be learned from the material systems that make my listening possible.

The new research question for my PDAP might therefore be: How has the material infrastructure through which I listen to music changed since 2018, and how has that shaped my listening?
